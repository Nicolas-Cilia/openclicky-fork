How to open OpenClicky

1. Drag OpenClicky to the Applications folder, then open it.
2. macOS says it "could not verify" OpenClicky, because the app is self-signed
   rather than notarized by Apple. Click Done.
3. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, and scroll down to Security.
4. Next to the message about OpenClicky, click Open Anyway.
5. Click Open Anyway again, then enter your password or use Touch ID.

You only do this once on each Mac.
On macOS 14, you can instead right-click the app and choose Open.
